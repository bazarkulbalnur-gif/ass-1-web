Project wireframe design, sitemap, and topic description for the group midterm project.

Project Overview: WanderLands (Travel Agency)

"WanderLands" is an interactive travel agency website designed to help users explore global destinations, discover popular tourist spots, and plan their dream vacations effortlessly. The target audience includes travel enthusiasts, students, and anyone looking for convenient tour packages and travel inspiration. The main purpose of the website is to provide a seamless, user-friendly platform where visitors can browse destinations, view detailed trip information, and easily get in touch with our agency.

Project Authors / Team: Balnur & Ayazhan

Sitemap Structure (5 Pages):

  • 1. Home Page: Main landing, welcome banner, and trending destinations.
  • 2. Destinations: Catalog of global countries and tourist spots with search/filters.
  • 3. Trip Details: Detailed view of selected location, guides, and tips.
  • 4. About Us: Information about our travel agency mission and team.
  • 5. Contact: Feedback form, social links, and office location.
