Nursultan Nazarbayev

The Life Journey and Career Milestones

🎵 Audio Track:

Main Historical Dates and Periods

The life journey and career of Nursultan Abishuly Nazarbayev can be briefly categorized into the following main historical dates and periods:

1. Early Life Period (1940s–1960s)

  • July 6, 1940: Born in the village of Ushkonyr, Karasay district, Almaty region.
  • 1960: Began his career at the construction site of the Kazakhstan Magnitka in Temirtau. Later worked at the Karaganda Metallurgical Combine (as a blast furnace assistant and metal caster).

2. Rise of Socio-Political Career (1960s–1980s)

  • 1967: Graduated from the higher technical educational institution attached to the Karaganda Metallurgical Combine in Temirtau.
  • 1969–1973: Engaged in party and Komsomol work in Temirtau.
  • 1973–1977: Served as the Secretary of the Party Committee of the Karaganda Metallurgical Combine.
  • 1977–1979: Served as Secretary and Second Secretary of the Karaganda Regional Committee of the Communist Party of Kazakhstan.
  • 1979–1984: Served as Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Kazakhstan.
  • 1984–1989: Served as Chairman of the Council of Ministers of the Kazakh SSR.
  • 1989–1991: First Secretary of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of Kazakhstan, and simultaneously Chairman of the Supreme Soviet of the Kazakh SSR starting February 1990.

3. Period of Independence and State Governance (1990–2019)

  • April 24, 1990: Elected President of the Kazakh SSR.
  • December 1, 1991: The first nationwide presidential election in Kazakhstan's history took place, and Nursultan Nazarbayev was elected President.
  • December 16, 1991: Signed the Constitutional Law "On the State Independence of the Republic of Kazakhstan."
  • August 29, 1991: Signed the historical decree closing the Semipalatinsk nuclear test site.
  • June 4, 1992: The new state symbols of the country (Flag, Emblem, Anthem) were approved.
  • November 15, 1993: The national currency—the Tenge—was introduced into circulation.
  • August 30, 1995: The current Constitution of the Republic of Kazakhstan was adopted through a nationwide referendum.
  • 1997: The decision to relocate the capital from Almaty to Akmola (now Astana) was implemented.
  • December 1, 2010: The OSCE Summit was held in Astana under Kazakhstan’s chairmanship.
  • 2011: Kazakhstan hosted the 7th Asian Winter Games.
  • 2017: The international specialized exhibition "EXPO-2017" was held in Astana.
  • March 19, 2019: Announced the termination of his powers as President of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
